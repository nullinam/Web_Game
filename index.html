Brand and home

The code mark and lowkey.exe logo return you to the top. The tagline introduces the arcade and the Enter Arcade button jumps to the complete game library.

Theme switch

Click the glowing theme button to cycle through Dark, Void, Neon, Asteroid, Sakura, and Copper. Your choice is remembered on this device; home-page atmosphere effects stay behind the games.

Game library cards

Each card shows the category, game icon, short description, filename, and Play Game action. The Local tag means the game files are part of this website; Offline means no online account is needed. Choose a card to launch its game in the integrated player.

Integrated game player

Choose Play Game to open a game in this tab. Use Back to Library or Close to return. Open in New Tab is available when you want the game on its own.

Settings

The gear opens the local settings card. Atmosphere effects can be switched off. Your theme and effects preference are saved in this browser.

Offline storage and play

Interface preferences are stored in browser storage. The games are files served by this website and do not require an account. Browser storage can be cleared from browser settings.
